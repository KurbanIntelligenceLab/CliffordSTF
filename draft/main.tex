\begin{abstract}
In three dimensions, the geometric product of two vectors realises only the $L{=}0$ and $L{=}1$ components of the Clebsch--Gordan decomposition $\mathbf{1} \otimes \mathbf{1} = \mathbf{0} \oplus \mathbf{1} \oplus \mathbf{2}$, leaving the five-dimensional symmetric traceless $L{=}2$ component structurally absent at the per-edge bilinear level. We identify this gap as the representation-theoretic reason that geometric-algebra interatomic potentials predict force magnitudes accurately but force directions poorly, and we close it with two explicit Cartesian primitives, a symmetric traceless rank-2 tensor ($\mathrm{STF}_2$) and its rank-3 derivative ($\mathrm{STF}_3$), operating alongside a $\mathrm{Cl}(3,0)$ multivector inside a single message-passing substrate. The resulting architecture, CliffordSTF, has one core bilinear, the geometric product implemented as an $8 \times 8$ Cayley table, augmented by closed-form symmetrisation and trace removal, and requires neither e3nn, Clebsch--Gordan tables, nor Wigner-$D$ coefficients. We show that the geometric product combined with closed-form STF operations spans the full Clebsch--Gordan decomposition for irrep tensor products through $L{=}3$ without CG tables, spherical harmonics, or Wigner-$D$ matrices, realising at the architectural level the classical Cartesian--spherical equivalence of irreducible tensors. We demonstrate the diagnosis mechanistically on rMD17 at matched parameter budget: adding the STF tracks to the base Clifford backbone raises aggregate force cosine similarity from $0.055$ to $0.551$, a tenfold directional gain, while simultaneously improving aggregate force MAE from $20.8$ to $17.6$\,meV/\AA{} and energy MAE from $34.1$ to $30.4$\,meV. A twelve-variant controlled ablation confirms that the CliffordSTF scaffold with all flags off already closes most of the directional gap, while a readout-only variant that places STF tracks only at the output retains near-vanilla cosine, isolating the dominant effect to an $L \geq 2$ substrate operating throughout message passing rather than at the readout alone. Trained on OC22 at under one million parameters, CliffordSTF improves on a fully-configured EquiformerV2 at $L{=}4$ on both in-distribution and out-of-distribution OC22 IS2RE (energy MAE $4.09$ and $6.46$\,eV versus $4.90$ and $6.62$\,eV for EquiformerV2) and attains the best out-of-distribution energy MAE in the study on OC22 S2EF ($5.08$\,eV). Two equivariant lineages traditionally treated as disjoint, geometric algebra and irreducible Cartesian tensors, are best understood as complementary, and their hybridisation removes a substantial portion of the e3nn dependency from competitive interatomic-potential design.
\end{abstract}
